Jingle Bells / The One Horse Open Sleigh
Composition: James Lord Pierpont (1822–1893), published 1857, Oliver Ditson & Co.
Historical score reference: Library of Congress, https://www.loc.gov/item/sm1857.451080/
Rights basis: original 1857 melody; author's death was 133 years before 2026.
This app's numeric monophonic transcription and bell accompaniment were made for Merry Village.
No external recording, MIDI performance, recent commercial arrangement or lyric translation was copied.
The numeric transcription, accompaniment and synthesized performance were newly prepared for this repository; no third-party performance is used.
The LOC link is a historical reference, not a third-party recording license. The scan was not bundled.
