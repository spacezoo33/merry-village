O Tannenbaum / O Christmas Tree
Composition: traditional German melody; Christmas song version published 1824 by Ernst Anschütz (1780–1861).
Rights basis: traditional melody, 1824 publication, author deceased 165 years before 2026.
This app's numeric melody transcription and bell accompaniment were made for Merry Village.
No external recording, MIDI performance, modern commercial arrangement or lyric translation was copied.
The numeric transcription, accompaniment and synthesized performance were newly prepared for this repository; no third-party performance is used.
No claim is made that any modern recording titled O Christmas Tree is public domain.
