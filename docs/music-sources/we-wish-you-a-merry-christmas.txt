We Wish You a Merry Christmas — traditional West Country carol melody

The app's monophonic MIDI pitch/duration transcription is in src/catalog.ts
(track id lullaby, G major, 112 BPM). The accompaniment and music-box synthesis
are newly written for Merry Village. No lyrics, recording, piano score, choral
parts, or modern commercial arrangement are included.

The traditional melody must be distinguished from Arthur Warrell's 1935
arrangement and modern recordings. Neither of those is used here.
A separately inspected melody reference identifies the tune as traditional:
https://github.com/robsoncouto/arduino-songs/blob/ae71b2e92c463bf93f320887b89f97374767efbb/merrychristmas/merrychristmas.ino
Its source code has no explicit repository licence; its program and score were
NOT copied or imported. It is a classification/cross-check reference only.
The app transcription was written independently before reading that reference.

The direct Hymnary source was blocked by this environment's network policy.
This record does not claim that the blocked page or any historic printed score
was examined, or that any modern arrangement/recording has been cleared.
